EmoBond Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

EmoBond ("we", "our") helps you strengthen your relationships through AI-powered coaching and insights. Your emotional data is deeply personal, and we treat it that way. This policy explains what we collect, how we collect it, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

What we collect and how

DataHow it's collectedHow it's used
Account information — email, name, optional phone number and profile photo You provide it when you sign up (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email) To create and secure your account. Handled via Supabase authentication.
Relationship data — communication entries, mood check-ins, harmony metrics, coaching transcripts You enter it in the app To generate your coaching, insights and progress. Stored with row-level security so only your account can read it.
Emotional check-in photos Captured by you with the camera, only when you start a check-in Sent to our AI provider to describe your apparent emotional state. Processed in memory and never stored on our servers.
Usage analytics Collected automatically as anonymized feature-usage events To understand which features are used and improve the app. We never send your message content, photos, or transcripts to analytics.
Partner link data — your tendency chart, daily mood ratings, reflections you choose to share, and the moments you add together Only if you link with a partner, and only for what you each switch on Shown to your linked partner. Your answers, coaching conversations and private notes are never shared. Either partner can end the link at any time, which stops all sharing immediately.
Push token (a device identifier for notifications) Only if you turn notifications on To deliver reminders and partner activity. Partner notifications contain only the type of event and your partner's first name — never a note, a mood or a score. Turn notifications off in Profile at any time; the token is removed when you sign out or delete your account.

AI processing and third-party sharing

Text coaching replies, insights, suggestions and check-in analyses are generated by Anthropic's Claude models, accessed through our secure backend.

What is sent to Anthropic: the text of the message you write, and, for an emotional check-in, the photo you capture. What is not sent: your name, email address, phone number, account identifiers, or your stored relationship history.

Your permission: before your first AI-powered session, EmoBond shows an in-app explanation of what is sent and to whom, and asks for your explicit consent. You can decline and continue using the non-AI parts of the app, and you can withdraw consent later from your Profile.

Protection by the third party: data is sent to Anthropic solely to generate your response. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models, and Anthropic is contractually required to provide protections for this data equal to those described in this policy.

Voice calls with EmoBond IQ are carried by OpenAI's Realtime API. During a call your voice is streamed to OpenAI to understand you and speak back, and a transcript is written for you into your own session history. Alongside the audio, our server sends OpenAI only the same computed summary of your Snapshot and shared patterns that the text coach sees — never your raw answers, name or contact details. Per OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train their models. Calls are limited to a few minutes each and to a monthly allowance, enforced on our server. You agree to this in the app before your first AI feature runs, and can withdraw in Profile.

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties.

What we never do

We never sell your data. We never share your relationship data with advertisers. We never store your check-in photos. Coaching is supportive guidance, not medical or psychological care.

Subscriptions

EmoBond Premium is processed by Apple or Google. We receive subscription status only — never your payment details.

Your rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, your data at any time. Delete your account in the app under Profile, or email isnsaka@gmail.com. Deletion removes your profile, relationship data and session history within 30 days.

Data security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is scoped per user with database row-level security. AI API keys are held server-side only and are never included in the app.

Children

EmoBond is intended for users 17 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this policy as the app evolves and note the revision date above.
