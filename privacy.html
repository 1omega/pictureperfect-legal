Privacy Policy

Glowra ("the App") — Last updated: July 23, 2026

Glowra is operated by Ijeoma Nsaka ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash) when you create an account.
  • Photos: facial photos you capture or upload for skin analysis.
  • Analysis results: AI-generated skin metrics derived from your photos (e.g. hydration, clarity, elasticity scores).
  • Wellness check-ins: optional sleep, stress, and mood entries you log.
  • Product interactions: products you view, save, or mark as in use.
  • Payment information: if you subscribe on the web, payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI skin analysis: your photos are securely transmitted to our AI analysis providers (Perfect Corp and Face++) solely to generate your skin metrics, then results are stored in your account.
  • To show your progress over time and personalize product and routine recommendations.
  • To operate your subscription and account.
  • To respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your photos for advertising, and we do not use them to train AI models.

Face Data

What face data we collect: the facial photos you capture or upload for skin analysis, and the AI-generated cosmetic appearance estimates derived from them (numeric "glow"/skin-appearance scores such as clarity, hydration, and elasticity estimates). We do not create or store facial-recognition templates or faceprints, and the App does not identify who you are, estimate your age, or judge your appearance.

How it is used: a facial photo is used solely to generate your cosmetic skin-appearance estimates. These estimates are for informational purposes only and are not a medical measurement, diagnosis, or treatment.

Who it is shared with: to generate your analysis, your facial photo is securely transmitted to our AI analysis providers, Perfect Corp and Face++ (Megvii), acting solely as our processors. They may process the photo only to return your analysis and may not use it for their own purposes. Face data is not shared with any other third party, is never sold, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.

Where it is stored: facial photos and their derived scores are stored in secured Supabase storage and database, protected by row-level security so only your account can access them. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

How long it is retained: facial photos and derived scores are retained so you can track changes over time, until you delete them or delete your account. When you request deletion, the data is removed from our systems within 30 days.

How to delete it: in the App under Profile → Privacy & Data, you can delete your photos, or delete your account and all associated data. You may also email us to request deletion.

Where Your Data Lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (our database and storage provider) with row-level security so only your account can access your records. Photos are stored in secured storage used only for generating your analysis.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Delete your photos: available in the App under Profile → Privacy & Data.
  • Delete your account and all data: also under Profile → Privacy & Data, or email us.
  • Access or correct your data: email us at the address below.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Not Medical Advice

Glowra provides AI-powered estimates for general wellness purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
