Delete Your Glowra Account

Glowra — AI Skin Analysis · Developer: Ijeoma Nsaka · Last updated: July 17, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Glowra account and the data associated with it. There are two ways to do it — either works, and both delete the same data.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Glowra and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Privacy & Data.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when prompted. Your account and associated data are deleted immediately.

If you only want to remove your photos but keep your account, use Delete My Photos on the same screen.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Email isnsaka@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Glowra account, with the subject line “Delete my account”.

We verify the request comes from the account owner and complete deletion within 30 days. We will confirm by email once it is done.

What gets deleted

Data typeWhat happens
Face photos and scan imagesDeleted permanently. Removed from storage and not recoverable.
Skin analysis results and historyDeleted permanently.
Name and email addressDeleted permanently.
Account credentialsDeleted permanently.
Beauty Chat conversation historyDeleted permanently.

What we keep, and for how long

Data typeRetentionWhy
Purchase and transaction records Up to 7 years Required for tax, accounting and financial-audit obligations. These records are held by our payment processor and contain no photos or skin-analysis data.
Backups containing deleted data Purged within 30 days Deleted data may persist briefly in encrypted routine backups before those backups roll over.

Before you delete

  • Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a Glowra Premium subscription. Cancel it first — via Google Play Subscriptions, the App Store, or the billing-portal link in your receipt email — otherwise it may continue to renew.
  • Account deletion is permanent. Your scan history and progress data cannot be restored afterwards.
