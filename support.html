Frequently Asked Questions

How do I run a skin scan?

Open the Scan tab and tap Activate Camera, or tap Upload Photo to use an existing photo. Results appear in a few seconds on the Analysis tab. For best accuracy use soft, even, natural light and keep your whole face in frame.

How many scans do I get?

Free accounts include 3 scans per month. Glowra Premium ($6.99/month) includes unlimited scans, the Beauty Chat AI assistant, and your full analysis history.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

If you subscribed on the web, payments are handled by Stripe — manage or cancel any time via the billing portal link in your receipt email, or email us. If you subscribed through the App Store, manage it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling stops future renewals; Premium stays active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my photos or my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Privacy & Data. You can delete your photos, or delete your account and all associated data. You can also email us and we will action it for you.

Are my photos used to train AI?

No. Your photos are used only to generate your own skin analysis. We do not sell your personal information, we do not use your photos for advertising, and we do not use them to train AI models. See our Privacy Policy for full detail.

Why do my results vary between scans?

Lighting, camera angle and time of day all affect the image the AI sees. For results that are comparable over time, scan in similar lighting and hold the camera at a similar distance each time.
